Giridih assembly (AC-32), Vidhan Sabha 2024, from the ECI Form 20 ("Total
Votes Polled" row: EVM votes at 367 polling stations plus postal ballots):

- Winner: Sudivya Kumar "Sonu" (JMM): **94,042** votes, 45.28% of valid votes
  (93,179 EVM + 863 postal)
- Runner-up: Nirbhay Kumar Shahabadi (BJP): **90,204**, 43.43% (89,341 EVM +
  863 postal)
- Margin: **3,838 votes (1.85%)**, the tightest of the three recent results
- Third: Navin Anand (JLKM): 10,787, 5.19%. Fourteen candidates stood. Eleven
  have no party in the loaded sources, because Form 20 prints names only.
  Twelve forfeited their deposits.
- Valid votes 2,07,682 (NOTA included); NOTA 2,004 (0.96%); rejected 139 (all
  postal); votes polled 2,07,821. EVM 2,05,777, postal 1,905.
- Electors 3,04,898 is a secondary published figure, since no roll is loaded.
  Turnout 68.16% rests on it.

**Booths against the result.** Shahabadi led 193 polling stations, Sudivya
Kumar 166 and Navin Anand 8. Sudivya Kumar won with fewer booths because his
leads, where he had them, were larger. Postal ballots split exactly evenly
between the two (863 each), so they did not decide the seat.

Trend: the margin was 9,933 (BJP) in 2014, 15,884 (JMM) in 2019 and 3,838
(JMM) in 2024. See the 2019 card for the swing between the two Form 20s.
Booth-level figures are in `mv_result_booth_wide` and
`/acs/32/elections/VS-2024/candidates`. Every row there cites its Form 20 page.
