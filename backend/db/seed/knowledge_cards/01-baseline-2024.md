Giridih assembly (AC-32), Vidhan Sabha 2024:

- Winner: Sudivya Kumar "Sonu" (JMM) - 94,042 votes, 45.3%
- Runner-up: Nirbhay Kumar Shahabadi (BJP) - 90,204 votes, 43.4%
- Margin: **3,838 votes (1.85%)** - the tightest recent result in this seat
- JLKM (Navin Anand): 10,787 / 5.2%; NOTA: 2,004
- Electors 3,04,898; turnout 68.1%

Trend: margin was 9,933 (BJP win) in 2014, 15,884 (JMM win) in 2019 and 3,838
(JMM win) in 2024. Electors grew about 15% between 2019 and 2024, so new-voter
analysis carries unusual weight in this seat.
