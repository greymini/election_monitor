Giridih assembly (AC-32), Vidhan Sabha 2019, from the ECI Form 20 (367 polling
stations plus postal ballots):

- Winner: Sudivya Kumar (JMM): **80,871**, 48.19% of valid votes
- Runner-up: Nirbhay Kumar Shahabadi (BJP): **64,987**, 38.72%
- Margin: **15,884 votes (9.46%)**
- Third: Chunnu Kant: 6,903, 4.11%, with no party in the loaded sources.
  Twelve candidates stood, and ten forfeited their deposits.
- Valid votes 1,67,822 (NOTA included); NOTA 2,773 (1.65%); rejected 71;
  votes polled 1,67,893; postal 271. Electors 2,64,814 (secondary).

**2019 to 2024.** The same two candidates led both times. Sudivya Kumar's share
fell from 48.19% to 45.28%, Shahabadi's rose from 38.72% to 43.43%, and the
margin narrowed from 15,884 to 3,838. In 2019 Sudivya Kumar led 189 polling
stations to Shahabadi's 178; in 2024 Shahabadi led 193 to 166.

How the booths are compared: the polling-station numbering is stable between
the two Form 20s. Votes at the same PS number correlate at r = 0.96, against
about 0.27 when offset by one or two. Each 2019 station is therefore linked
to the 2024 booth with the same number (confidence 0.95, unreviewed, evidence
in `crosswalk_audit`). Booth swing is in `mv_swing`. It should be reviewed
against the PS list once that is loaded.
