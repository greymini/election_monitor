## Panchayats: where they come from

The panchayat list is the Local Government Directory's (`pri_local_bodies`), not
names anyone typed: seeding invented names would silently corrupt the geography
every rollup depends on. `scripts/build_panchayats.py` builds the three files
above from LGD extracts in `raw/geo/lgd/`.

- **Hindi names.** LGD has none for Jharkhand panchayats, and automatic
  transliteration got 0 of 8 known names right. Eight Pirtand panchayats take
  their Hindi name from the polling-station list (the namesake village as
  printed there), and its matched villages are Hindi aliases; every other
  panchayat's `name_hi` repeats the English name until a Hindi source (the
  Jharkhand SEC panchayat list) is added.
- **Which constituency.** Sources disagree for AC-32 (DECISIONS D-012). Each
  panchayat goes to the constituency most of its villages are in by LGD's
  village `ac_no`, and the `membership` column says how sure that is. Twelve-odd
  Giridih-block panchayats therefore sit in Gandey (31) under "Giridih Block (part)".
- **Not covered:** Nawadih and Chandrapura (Bokaro), Tundi, Purvi Tundi and
  Topchanchi (Dhanbad), and the Ranchi blocks of Silli and Kanke - only Giridih
  district was extracted.
- The polling-station list remains the authority for which booth sits in which
  panchayat (`ingest/parse_pslist.py`); it should agree with this, and where it
  does not, it wins.

## Caveat on `surname_dict.csv`

This dictionary drives an **estimate**, not a fact. Surnames map to communities
only probabilistically; ambiguous ones (Singh, Kumar, Das, Sinha) carry
`weight < 1` and are split, and some are deliberately left unmapped. Nothing in
this file is applied to an individual voter: `parse_roll.py` counts surnames into
per-booth aggregates and discards the names (HLD §5, LLD §12). Every figure it
produces must be shown with its confidence band and reviewed before use.
